# Charlie's Train 🚂

A mouse-driven train game made for a 3½-year-old.

**Play it:** https://lukemerrett.github.io/charlies-train-game/

## How to play

- **Green ▶** — go!
- **Red ■** — stop. Press it as the next station comes up (the button pulses) and the train glides to the platform.
- **Yellow 📯** — toot toot!

At a station, passengers hop off and new ones hop on. Click a passenger on the platform to put them on the train, or click one on the train to let them off.

Lots of things react when you click them: animals in the fields, trees (sometimes a bird flies out), houses, the station sign, ducks and jumping fish under the bridge, clouds (it rains!) and the sun.

The track goes on for ever, through farms, forests, towns, tunnels and over rivers.

The small buttons in the top-left corner turn the sound on or off and switch to full screen.

## Tech

It's one self-contained `index.html` file: canvas graphics, emoji characters and Web Audio sounds, with no images, libraries or build step. Open the file in any browser to play offline.
