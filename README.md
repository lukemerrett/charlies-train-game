# Charlie's Spooky Train 🎃🚂👻

A mouse-driven Halloween model railway, made for a 3½-year-old.

**Play it:** https://lukemerrett.github.io/charlies-train-game/

## How to play

- **Green ▶**: go!
- **Red ■**: stop. Press it as the next station comes up (the button pulses and the station sign glows), and the train glides to the platform.
- **Purple 👻**: the spooky ghost whistle. Whoo-oooo!

The whole layout is shown from above at an angle, like a model railway on a table. On small screens, such as phones, the camera follows the train instead.

There are four stations: the Pumpkin Patch, the Haunted House, Witch Wood and Bat Castle. At each one, passengers (ghosts, witches, vampires, black cats, bats…) hop off and new ones hop on. Click someone on the platform to put them on the train, or click a passenger to let them off.

Lots of things react when you click them:
- the ghosts floating around
- jack-o'-lanterns
- the graveyard's tombstones (a little ghost pops out)
- the witch and her bubbling cauldron
- dead trees (bats fly out)
- the owl and the dangling spider
- the frog in the pond, the black cat and the wolf on the tunnel hill
- station houses (the lights switch off and on), lamps and the bats flying past
- the moon (a wolf howls)

The train goes through a tunnel and over a bridge.

The small buttons in the top-left corner turn the sound on or off and switch to full screen.

## Tech

It's one self-contained `index.html` file: isometric canvas graphics, emoji characters and Web Audio sounds, with no images, libraries or build step. The title font comes from Google Fonts. Open the file in any browser to play offline.
